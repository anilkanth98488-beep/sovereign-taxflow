# Sovereign TaxFlow — Freelance Launch Kit

## 1. Fixed-Fee Service Offer (1-Page Pitch)

**Service:** Pre-Tax & Monthly Workpaper Cleanup Packet  
**Target Buyer:** Small CPA firms, Enrolled Agents (EAs), and bookkeepers  
**What You Deliver (Fixed Fee per Client Packet):**
1. **Reconciled Bank/Ledger Tie-Out** (PASS-verified, formula-sanitized `.xlsx` workpaper).
2. **Trial Balance & Book-to-Tax Exception Report** (flags out-of-balance differences, `REVIEW_REQUIRED` §162(f)/meals items, and M-3 thresholds).
3. **Payroll & 1099/W-2 Tie-Out Summary** (Q1–Q4 Form 941 vs. W-3/W-2 and 1099-INT/DIV/NEC/R vs. draft return, plus ready-to-send preparer follow-up questions).
4. **15-Minute Reviewer Walkthrough**.

**Scope Boundary (Include in Every Proposal):**
> *"Sovereign TaxFlow provides deterministic workpaper preparation, arithmetic tie-outs, and exception reporting. Your firm's licensed CPA or EA retains full authority and responsibility for all tax positions, elections, and return filings."*
